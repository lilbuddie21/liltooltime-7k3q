Quick tips

  • Home Depot: Shop All, then Savings, then Shop All Savings shows price-reduced items.
  • In a store, read the markdown date on the clearance tag. Items often drop again every few weeks.
  • On HiBid, filter by distance first. Bulky tools are often pickup only.
  • Look up recent sold or listed prices for the exact model before you bid.
